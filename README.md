# ship-easy
An intelligent shipping decision system that combines historical data and real-time disruptions to predict shipment risk and recommend whether to ship, hold, or reroute.

Product Information:

Ship-Easy is a decision-support and predictive platform for individuals and organizations shipping packages overseas. In 2026 alone, 435 billion parcels are expected to be shipped internationally, more than any year prior; that's 13,800 packages delivered every second! However, in December 2025, approximately 37.6% of international packages were delayed from their intended shipping day. While the number of packages shipped overseas keeps increasing, current shipment systems like freighters/cargo ships still experience systemic delays such as bottleneck movements in major ports, and some unaccounted delays such as environmental factors (natural disasters, ocean current, weather), strikes, and political events. 

Ship-Easy aims to provide greater transparency and power over the blacbox of international shipping. 66% of consumers reported that while they understand international shipments may be delayed, they want greater transparency into why their shipment was delayed. 

Ship-Easy uses a modern full-stack architecture to turn shipping uncertainty into actionable insight: a React frontend for a clean decision-focused dashboard, a FastAPI backend for fast, reliable API access, and PostgreSQL for structured shipment, product, and historical risk data. On the backend, the system combines statistical baseline predictions with real-time disruption signals and configurable product risk profiles, then applies a lightweight recommendation layer to suggest whether a shipment should ship now, wait, reroute, or move to expedited handling while also explaining reasons why the shipping is delayed. This makes the platform more than a delay tracker for shippers: it’s a transparent decision-support engine that explains not only what happened, but what to do next. For consumers, the platform provides the accuracy and transparency needed to understand international shipping better.
